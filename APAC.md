# APAC launch kit: JPX and HKEX on Databento

Databento has registered five APAC datasets in its open-source [`dbn`](https://github.com/databento/dbn) library:

| Dataset | Venue | Added to `dbn` |
|---|---|---|
| `XTKS.FLEX` | Tokyo Stock Exchange (cash equities) | 2026-08-03 |
| `XOSE.ITCH` | Osaka Exchange (Nikkei 225 / TOPIX futures and options) | 2026-08-03 |
| `XTKT.ITCH` | Tokyo Commodity Exchange (energy) | 2026-08-03 |
| `XHKG.OMDC` | HKEX securities | 2026-11-16 ("Unreleased") |
| `XHKF.OMDD` | HKEX futures | 2026-11-16 ("Unreleased") |

On 2026-11-24, the historical API returned `dataset_not_found` for all five. The [Series C post](https://databento.com/blog/series-c) says data-center buildouts at HKEX and JPX are nearly complete.

This page is what I'd want open on launch day: when each venue's trading day starts and ends in UTC, the tickets Asian-hours customers are likely to file, and a checklist to verify all of it against the real data in the first hour.

All exchange facts come from JPX and HKEX pages (links below), checked 2026-11-24. Anything about how Databento will represent these venues is marked **verify at launch**, because nobody outside Databento can see that data yet.

## 1. The one thing to know: a UTC day is not a trade date here

Databento's `ohlcv-1d` bars and date-only `start`/`end` cover UTC days. On CME, that already moves the daily open on 34 of 35 days (see the [measured study](findings/daily-bars-vs-sessions.md)). Osaka and HKEX futures are worse, because their night sessions belong to the **next** trade date.

**What one UTC-day bar (2026-12-01 00:00 to 24:00 UTC) contains**, computed by `sessions.utc_day_contents()`:

| Venue | Minutes from trade date 1 Dec | Minutes from trade date 2 Dec | What that means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Osaka (XOSE) | 410 of 425 in the day session (09:00 onwards; it **misses the 08:45 opening auction**) | the **whole night session** (785 min) plus 2 Dec's opening auction (15 min) | Most of the bar belongs to 2 Dec. Its open is not the JPX open, and its close is the 06:00 JST night close. |
| HKEX futures (XHKF) | the whole day session (440 min) | the **whole after-hours T+1 session** (605 min) | The bar's close is the 03:00 HKT after-hours close, not the 16:30 day close. |
| Tokyo stocks (XTKS) | all 335 min | 0 | Clean. The 09:00 JST open is exactly 00:00 UTC. |
| HKEX stocks (XHKG) | all 370 min | 0 | Clean. |

**Fix to give customers:** rebuild daily bars from `ohlcv-1m` by trade date with `sessions.session_bars(df, "XOSE")`, and request one trade date with `sessions.trade_window("XOSE", date)`.

## 2. Sessions in local time and UTC

These are generated by `sessions.session_table()`. Session ends are padded 5 minutes so closing-auction prints are included. "Orders only" phases have no trades.

**Tokyo Stock Exchange, trade date Tue 1 Dec 2026** (JST = UTC+9, no DST)

| Session | JST | UTC |
|---|---|---|
| pre-open (orders only) | 08:00–09:00 | **Mon 30 Nov** 23:00–00:00 |
| morning | 09:00–11:30 | 00:00–02:30 |
| lunch | 11:30–12:30 | 02:30–03:30 |
| afternoon + closing auction 15:25–15:30 | 12:30–15:30 | 03:30–06:30 |

**Osaka Exchange (index futures), trade date Tue 1 Dec 2026.** For Monday's trade date, the night session starts on **Friday**.

| Session | JST | UTC |
|---|---|---|
| night pre-open (orders only) | Mon 16:45–17:00 | Mon 07:45–08:00 |
| night | Mon 17:00 – Tue 06:00 | Mon 08:00–21:00 |
| day pre-open (orders only) | Tue 08:00–08:45 | **Mon** 23:00–23:45 |
| day (closing auction 15:45) | Tue 08:45–15:45 | **Mon 23:45** – Tue 06:45 |

**HKEX securities, trade date Tue 1 Dec 2026** (HKT = UTC+8)

| Session | HKT | UTC |
|---|---|---|
| pre-opening session (auction matches at a random time 09:20–09:22) | 09:00–09:30 | 01:00–01:30 |
| morning | 09:30–12:00 | 01:30–04:00 |
| lunch | 12:00–13:00 | 04:00–05:00 |
| afternoon | 13:00–16:00 | 05:00–08:00 |
| closing auction (random close 16:08–16:10) | 16:00–16:10 | 08:00–08:10 |

## 3. Schedules change, and history must use the one in force

| Date | Change | Effect on historical data |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-09-21 | OSE night session close 05:30 → 06:00 (J-GATE 3.0) | |
| 2024-11-05 | TSE close 15:00 → 15:30, with a new closing auction 15:25–15:30 | Bars before this date end 30 minutes earlier. This is not missing data. |
| 2024-11-05 | OSE day close 15:15 → 15:45; night open 16:30 → 17:00 | |
| 2026-07-20 | HKEX after-hours open 17:15 → 17:00 | |
| proposed, Mar 2027 | HKEX after-hours close 03:00 → 04:15 (subject to approval) | not in `sessions.py` until confirmed |
| 2027-03-01 | TSE tick sizes assigned by liquidity instead of TOPIX500 membership | `min_price_increment` changes for many stocks |
| 2027-10-12 (may move) | TSE random close (15:29:30–15:30) | the close print time varies |

`sessions.py` applies each schedule from its effective date. A schedule change counts from the day the session *starts*, so a Friday-night session uses Friday's schedule.

## 4. Tickets to expect, with replies

**A. "My Nikkei 225 futures daily bar doesn't match JPX."**
The bar covers a UTC day, so it starts at 09:00 JST and contains the next trade date's night session (section 1). Rebuild with `session_bars(df, "XOSE")`. The official settlement is a different number from the close; use the `statistics` schema (**verify at launch** how OSE settlement and `ts_ref` are represented; on CME, `ts_ref` is the trade date at 00:00 UTC).

**B. "Data for Friday night shows up under Monday" / "Monday's data starts on Friday."**
That's correct for OSE and HKEX futures. Night and after-hours sessions belong to the next business day: Friday 17:00 JST counts toward Monday ([JPX](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/derivatives/rules/trading-hours/01.html), [HKEX](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Global/Exchange/FAQ/Derivatives-Market/Trading/After-Hours-Trading-(AHT)?sc_lang=en)).

**C. "There are trades on a Japanese holiday."**
OSE has holiday trading for index and commodity futures and options, and those trades count toward the next business day. There is no holiday trading on 31 Dec or 2 Jan. JPX was also fully closed on 23 Nov 2026 and will be on 20 Sep 2027 for business-continuity tests ([JPX](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/derivatives/rules/holidaytrading/index.html)).

**D. "Hong Kong data on a typhoon day? I thought the market closed."**
Since 23 Sep 2024, HKEX keeps trading under Typhoon Signal No. 8 and Black Rainstorm warnings, including after-hours ([HKEX](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Services/Trading-hours-and-Severe-Weather-Arrangements/Severe-Weather-Arrangements?sc_lang=en)).

**E. "One-minute bars are missing every day around noon."**
That's lunch:
- TSE: 11:30–12:30 JST (02:30–03:30 UTC)
- HKEX stocks: 12:00–13:00 HKT (04:00–05:00 UTC)
- HKEX futures: 12:00–13:00 HKT, with a pre-open from 12:30

No trades means no bar.

**F. "Symbol 5 / 700 isn't found" or "00700 isn't found."**
HKEX codes are numbers from 1 to 99999. The OMD-C feed sends them as integers, and the leading zeros are only display. **Verify at launch** which form `raw_symbol` uses, and put both in the docs. The same family of bug already exists: [databento-python#146](https://github.com/databento/databento-python/issues/146) shows the reference API turning a Hong Kong `00005` into `5`.

**G. "Why are there two instruments for the same company?"**
These are HKD–RMB dual counters (since 19 Jun 2023). For example, `00016` and `80016` are the same company on two currency counters, with different prices and currencies ([HKEX](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Services/Trading/Securities/Overview/Trading-Mechanism/HKD-RMB-Dual-Counter-Model?sc_lang=en)).

**H. "My code crashes on TSE symbol 130A."**
Since 1 Jan 2024, new TSE codes can have a letter in position 2 or 4: still 4 characters, using 19 letters (no B, E, I, O, Q, V or Z). Anything that stores codes as integers, or lets pandas infer the type, breaks. Read the codes as strings ([JPX](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/sicc/code-pr/p6b22i0000002skh-att/code-pr_1.pdf)).

**I. "Prices jump in odd steps" / "Why .005?"**
- HKEX uses a price-banded spread table: 0.001 up to HK$0.25, 0.005 up to HK$10, 0.01 up to HK$20, and so on ([HKEX Second Schedule](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Rules-and-Forms-and-Fees/Rules/SEHK/Securities/Rules/Sch_2_eng.pdf)).
- TSE uses a finer table for TOPIX500 stocks (¥0.1 up to ¥1,000) than for others (¥1 up to ¥3,000) ([JPX](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/trading/domestic/07.html)).

**J. "Trading stopped for 10 minutes. Is your feed down?"**
Probably not:
- OSE: a static circuit breaker halts all contract months for at least 10 minutes when price limits (±8%, then ±12%, then ±16%) are hit. Temporary halts also happen when an order would trade outside the dynamic range ([JPX](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/derivatives/rules/price-limit-cb/index.html)).
- HKEX: VCM starts a 5-minute cooling-off period ([HKEX VCM](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Trading/Securities/Overview/Trading-Mechanism/POS-and-VCM-Enhancement-Initiatives/Trading-Mechanism-for-VCM_2026_E.pdf)).

Check the `status` schema before escalating (**verify at launch** which events are published).

**K. "The closing price time is different every day."**
HKEX's closing auction ends at a random time between 16:08 and 16:10. The pre-open auction matches between 09:20 and 09:22. TSE moves to a random close from 12 Oct 2027.

**L. "Half-day? Where is the afternoon?"**
HKEX has half-days on the eves of Christmas, New Year and Lunar New Year: stocks have a closing auction at 12:00, and index futures close at 12:30. There is **no after-hours session that evening**, so the next trade date has none either. JPX has no half-days.

**M. "My order-book replay doesn't match the TSE opening price."**
Pre-open order entry starts at 08:00 JST, which is 23:00 UTC the **previous** day. A request with `start=<date>` misses the book-building that sets the 09:00 opening auction. Start from `trade_window("XTKS", date)[0]`.

**N. "The HK open moved by an hour for me."**
Asia has no daylight saving, but the US and Europe do. For a New York user, HKEX opens at 20:30 ET in winter and 21:30 ET in summer.

**O. "Contract expired early" / "HSI futures stopped at 16:00."**
- HSI futures: on its last trading day (the second-last business day of the month), the expiring month trades 13:00–16:00 only, with no after-hours session.
- Nikkei 225 futures: the last trading day is the business day before the second Friday, and the final settlement (SQ) comes from the next day's opening prices.

## 5. Launch-day checklist (first hour on real data)

1. `metadata.get_dataset_range` and `list_schemas` for each new dataset. Note the start date.
2. `symbology.resolve` for `00700`, `700`, `80016`, `130A`, `7203` and Nikkei 225 front-month futures. Record which forms resolve. Update tickets F and H.
3. Pull `definition` for one HSI and one Nikkei 225 future: `raw_symbol` format, `min_price_increment`, `expiration`, `currency`.
4. Pull one day of `ohlcv-1m` for each venue, then run `session_bars()` against `ohlcv-1d`. Repeat the CME study for XOSE and XHKF.
5. `statistics`: is settlement published, with what `stat_type`, and does `ts_ref` follow the CME convention?
6. `status`: are lunch breaks, VCM, circuit breakers and auction phases visible?
7. One holiday-trading day on OSE and one half-day on HKEX: check `trade_window()` against real data.
8. Live: from KL, connect to `xose-itch.lsg.databento.com` and `xhkf-omdd.lsg.databento.com` (if those hostnames follow the existing pattern) and record connect and auth times with `dbdoctor --live`.
9. Update this page and `sessions.py` with whatever differs, then post a short "what changed" note in the community.

- **HKEX 2026:** 1 Jan · 17–19 Feb · 3, 6, 7 Apr · 1, 25 May · 19 Jun · 1 Jul · 1, 19 Oct · 25 Dec. Half-days 16 Feb, 24 Dec, 31 Dec. 2027 is included too. ([HKEX calendar](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Services/Trading/Derivatives/Overview/Trading-Calendar-and-Holiday-Schedule?sc_lang=en))
